## The Core Problem We Solve
- 62% of calls to small businesses go to voicemail — most of those callers never call back, they just call the next business on the list.
- Most small-business websites are "digital business cards" — visitors look around and leave, with no path that pushes them to actually book or buy.
- Leads who don't convert immediately are usually just forgotten — no follow-up system.

## AI Receptionist (the core product)
An intelligent voice agent wired directly into the business's phone line. It:
- Answers every call, 24/7 — including nights, weekends, and while staff are with other customers
- Qualifies leads and answers common questions
- Books appointments directly into the calendar
- Escalates to a real person when it can't help
- Supports natural, multilingual conversation

## AI Voice Agents — two forms
1. **On the business's own website**: a voice assistant visitors can talk to directly in the browser, no phone call needed.
2. **On the business's real phone line**: the AI Receptionist described above, answering actual incoming calls.

## Appointment Booking
Bookings sync to a real, live calendar — customers (or callers) can book, reschedule, or cancel themselves without playing phone tag. Confirmations go out by email automatically.

## Missed Call Automation
This is the AI Receptionist's core job: instead of a missed call going to voicemail (where most people simply hang up and move on), the AI answers immediately, every time.
